# Product Mission

## Pitch

To-Done is a simple to-do list application that helps individuals manage their daily tasks by providing a clean, frictionless interface for creating, updating, and completing to-do items without the overhead of account creation or authentication.

## Users

### Primary Customers
- **Individuals**: Anyone who needs a quick, no-fuss way to track personal tasks
- **Casual Users**: People who want a lightweight task manager without commitment to complex productivity systems

### User Personas

**Busy Professional** (25-45)
- **Role:** Office worker, freelancer, or remote employee
- **Context:** Juggles multiple small tasks throughout the day
- **Pain Points:** Existing to-do apps require sign-up, have too many features, or are slow to load
- **Goals:** Quickly jot down tasks and check them off without friction

**Student** (18-30)
- **Role:** College or graduate student
- **Context:** Needs to track assignments, errands, and daily responsibilities
- **Pain Points:** Doesn't want another account to manage; needs something simpler than full project management tools
- **Goals:** A quick capture tool for tasks that just works

## The Problem

### Task Management Friction
Most to-do applications require account creation, email verification, and complex onboarding before users can add their first task. This creates unnecessary friction for people who simply want to write down what they need to do.

**Our Solution:** To-Done eliminates all barriers to entry. Open the app, add a task, and get back to work. No sign-up, no login, no complexity.

## Differentiators

### Zero Authentication
Unlike Todoist, Things, or other popular task managers, To-Done requires no account creation. Users can immediately start managing tasks without any registration process. This results in instant productivity with zero onboarding friction.

### Simplicity First
Unlike feature-rich alternatives that offer projects, labels, priorities, due dates, and integrations, To-Done focuses on the core task management workflow: add, update, complete, and delete. This results in a faster, more focused experience for users who don't need advanced features.

## Key Features

### Core Features
- **Add Tasks:** Quickly create new to-do items with a simple text input
- **Update Tasks:** Edit task descriptions to refine or correct details
- **Delete Tasks:** Remove tasks that are no longer needed
- **Complete Tasks:** Mark tasks as done to track progress

### User Experience Features
- **Instant Access:** No authentication means users can start immediately
- **Clean Interface:** Minimal design that puts focus on the tasks themselves
- **Persistent Storage:** Tasks are saved to the database and persist across sessions
